Dedicated to Selfless Service & Human Dignity

Mahadev Ashram Charitable Trust was established in Noida with one resolute commitment: to bridge the gap between resources and those in need through pure, transparent ground action.

OUR GENESIS & ETHOS

Born from Ground Reality, Driven by Devotion to Humanity

In peri-urban pockets of Noida and surrounding rural villages, countless families struggle with basic access to primary healthcare, children's school supplies, and food security. While policies exist, the ground reality often leaves destitute elders, daily wage workers, and bright young students unattended.

To confront these challenges directly, **President Mohit Sharma** together with like-minded trustees registered **Mahadev Ashram Charitable Trust**. The organization was established not as a distant corporate entity, but as a hands-on community seva family anchored in Noida Sector-115.

Every mission—from running 24x7 Kanwar pilgrim medical relief shelters to distributing Vidya scholarship bags and conducting free health checkups—is funded with 100% accountability, voluntary trustee leadership, and zero commercial motivation.

"True seva requires no fanfare. When you feed a hungry pilgrim, provide medicine to an ailing elder, or give books to an eager child, you serve the divine within humanity."

— Mohit Sharma, President

Our Vision & Mission

Guiding our operational roadmap today, tomorrow, and for generations to come.

Our Vision

A self-reliant and compassionate society where economic hardship does not hinder human dignity, basic healthcare, or intellectual growth. We envision resilient communities where every vulnerable child has a pen in hand, every elder is honored with care, and humanitarian crises are met with immediate collective solidarity.

  • ✓ Universal primary education for slum & rural kids
  • ✓ Zero-cost preventative healthcare at doorsteps
  • ✓ Sustainable self-help micro-livelihoods for women

Our Mission

To execute direct, uncompromised grassroots interventions with absolute fiscal transparency. We deploy dedicated teams for free multi-specialty health camps, distribute comprehensive Vidya study kits, provide monthly dry rations to neglected seniors, and manage round-the-clock pilgrim relief centers with unconditional love.

  • ✓ 100% direct-to-ground fund utilization
  • ✓ Zero religious or caste-based discrimination
  • ✓ 24x7 readiness for local emergencies & disasters
STANDARDS OF EXCELLENCE

The Pillars That Govern Our Trust

01

Absolute Transparency

All bank transfers route through our verified Canara Bank account with public audit records and direct beneficiary verification.

02

Nishkama Seva

Trustees and core coordinators serve on a 100% voluntary, honorary basis without drawing salaries or administrative percentages.

03

Ground-First Delivery

We avoid excessive administrative overhead. Rations, medicines, and kits are distributed directly into the hands of real families.

04

Compassionate Dignity

Whether washing feet of fatigued Kanwar pilgrims or guiding children in study circles, seva is performed with humility and reverence.
