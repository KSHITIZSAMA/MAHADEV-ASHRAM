Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Mahadev Ashram Charitable Trust ("Trust", "we", "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal data of our donors, volunteers, beneficiaries, and visitors. This policy outlines our collection, utilization, and safeguarding practices in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable Indian laws.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only when voluntarily provided by you:

  • Personal Identification: Full Name, Email Address, Contact Number, Mailing Address.
  • Donation & Transaction Records: Transaction reference IDs, UPI VPAs, bank deposit slips, and PAN numbers (as required for statutory tax receipting).
  • Volunteer Submissions: Skills, availability, location, and areas of service interest.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify and confirm charitable contributions and generate official tax acknowledgments.
  • To share transparent progress updates, annual seva reports, and healthcare camp schedules.
  • To coordinate volunteer mobilization during emergency food drives and Kanwar pilgrim shelters.
  • We never sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties or commercial marketers.

4. Security of Data

All sensitive payment data is handled directly by verified banking channels (Canara Bank and NPCI UPI gateway). Our website does not store card details, CVVs, or UPI PINs. Electronic records are maintained with strict access controls restricted to authorized trustees.
