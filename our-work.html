Grassroots Seva & Community Action

From free rural medical checkup camps to child literacy scholarships, elderly nutrition, and round-the-clock pilgrim relief in Noida.

SEVA PROGRAM PORTFOLIO

Active Community Programs

Healthcare Camp

Free Community Medical & Eye Camps

Conducting scheduled health clinics in peri-urban slums and rural Noida. Qualified volunteer physicians provide primary consultations, vitals monitoring, diabetes/BP checkups, and free generic medications.

  • • Free prescription medicines provided
  • • Specialized pediatric and geriatric care
  • • Preventative health hygiene education

Vidya Initiative

Vidya Scholarship & Study Kit Distribution

Ensuring no child drops out due to lack of study supplies. We distribute complete educational backpacks, notebook sets, geometry boxes, and seasonal school uniforms to students from daily wage families.

  • • Full school supplies kit per student
  • • Focus on girl child literacy & retention
  • • Evening community tuition circles
Sponsor A Child's Vidya (₹1,100) →
Elderly Welfare

Monthly Ration & Dignity for Seniors

Standing beside destitute seniors, widows, and persons with disabilities. We provide recurring monthly dry rations (atta, dal, rice, mustard oil, tea) along with essential winter blankets and compassionate companionship.

  • • Monthly survival grocery hampers
  • • Essential chronic medication assistance
  • • Dignity visits by youth volunteers
Sponsor Senior Ration (₹2,500) →
24x7 Kanwar Seva

24x7 Kanwar Pilgrim Shelter & Annadanam

During the month of Shravan, running round-the-clock rest camps on the Noida pilgrimage route. We provide nutritious hot meals (bhandara), clean water, blister foot care, antiseptic dressings, and massage reflexology.

  • • Over 15,000 pilgrims served annually
  • • Dedicated first-aid and medical staff
  • • Hygienic 24-hour food distribution
Support Annadanam Seva →
Community Feasts

Festival Seva & Maa Durga Blessings

Celebrating Navratri, Janmashtami, and religious festivals through open community feasts (Halwa & Puri vitran), warm clothes distribution, and sharing joyful devotion with the most vulnerable children and elders.

  • • Pure sattvic freshly cooked prasad
  • • Blankets and clothing drives
  • • Community harmony across all backgrounds
Sponsor A Bhandara Drive →
Women Empowerment

Vocational Tailoring & Skill Development

Equipping rural and slum women with sewing, tailoring, and handicraft training. By providing vocational skills, we empower mothers and young women to earn an independent livelihood and uplift their families.

  • • Free sewing machines upon certification
  • • Self-help group financial literacy
  • • Direct pathway to sustainable income
