WHO WE ARE

Mahadev Ashram Charitable Trust is a legally registered non-profit organization established to serve humanity without bias or discrimination. Guided by the eternal Vedic motto "Seva Paramo Dharmah", our work is rooted in transforming lives through healthcare access, children's education, elderly dignity, and community welfare.

Led by President Mohit Sharma and a board of committed trustees, our team works directly on the ground. 100% of public donations directly fund medicines for rural clinics, school books for needy children, and hot meals for pilgrims and destitute seniors.

NEED

Across rural and semi-urban pockets of Noida and NCR, thousands of families lack access to primary healthcare. Simple, treatable conditions like hypertension, eye infections, and chronic fever often turn severe simply because hospital visits are financially out of reach.

Simultaneously, hundreds of bright children are forced to discontinue their education due to the inability to afford basic textbooks, stationery kits, and school uniforms. Abandoned elderly individuals and destitute widows often spend days without warm nutrition or medical attention.

Mahadev Ashram Charitable Trust was founded to eliminate these disparities. We organize free community health camps, distribute Vidya study supplies, provide elderly ration kits, and run 24x7 Kanwar pilgrim shelter and Annadanam camps.
